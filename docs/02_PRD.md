# Product Overview

MailBrain is an AI-powered email productivity platform designed to help users intelligently manage their email accounts.

Unlike traditional email clients that simply display emails, MailBrain understands email content, identifies important messages, tracks pending replies, organizes emails automatically, summarizes long emails, suggests actions, and improves user productivity.

MailBrain works as an intelligent assistant on top of existing email services instead of replacing them.

---

# Problem Statement

Modern users receive hundreds or even thousands of emails.

Common problems include:

- Missing important emails
- Forgetting to reply
- Losing track of job applications
- Difficulty organizing emails
- Email overload
- Spam and phishing emails
- Time wasted searching for information
- Missing deadlines hidden inside emails

Existing email clients provide storage and communication but offer limited assistance in understanding and managing email effectively.

---

# Vision

Build an intelligent email productivity platform that helps users spend less time managing emails and more time acting on important information.

---

# Mission

Reduce email overload using intelligent automation while keeping users fully in control of every important decision.

# Product Goals

MailBrain should:

- Organize emails automatically.
- Detect important emails.
- Track pending replies.
- Summarize emails.
- Suggest actions.
- Improve productivity.
- Protect users from phishing.
- Provide intelligent search.
- Learn user preferences over time.
