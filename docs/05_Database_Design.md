# MailBrain Database Design

## Version

0.1

---

# Core Entities

## 1. User

Represents a person using MailBrain.

---

## 2. Email Account

Stores connected email accounts (Gmail initially).

---

## 3. Email

Stores metadata and analysis related to emails.

---

## 4. Category

Represents email categories such as:

- Work
- Personal
- Finance
- Shopping
- Social
- Promotions
- Spam

---

## 5. AI Summary

Stores summaries generated for emails.

---

## 6. Task

Stores tasks extracted from emails.

Example:

- Submit assignment
- Attend interview
- Pay electricity bill

---

## 7. Follow-Up

Tracks emails waiting for replies.

Examples:

- Job applications
- Internship applications
- Customer support
- Scholarship applications

---

## 8. Reminder

Stores reminders generated from emails.

---

## 9. Notification

Stores notifications shown to the user.

---

## 10. User Preferences

Stores application settings.

Examples:

- Theme
- Notification settings
- AI preferences
